4- Work Plan
-
Phase 1: Campaign Strategy Brief & Audience Research

Activities: Define campaign goals, target product categories, offer details, target platforms, and direct call-to-action (CTA). Analyze audience interest patterns and competitor ad structures using Google Trends and Meta Ads Library

Target Audience: Primary focus on Egyptian mothers aged 30–50 in New Cairo and the 5th Settlement; secondary focus on Egyptian households interested in decluttering, saving money, repair, donation, or resale

Key Deliverable: Structured campaign brief and audience research notes

Phase 2: AI Strategy & Messaging Routes

Activities: Utilize AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini) to synthesize research, develop creative visual directions, and draft message variations. Human review ensures empathetic Egyptian Arabic tone and cultural fit

Key Deliverable: Approved creative direction and campaign copy variants

Phase 3: Prompt Engineering & Asset Creation 

Activities: Formulate and refine reusable image generation prompts. Produce 3–5 campaign-ready visual assets and at least 3 reusable templates in Canva/Figma, with optional short video/motion edits using CapCut .

Key Deliverable: AI prompt library, initial visual assets, and editable social media templates.

Phase 4: Brand Review & Quality Approval

Activities: Evaluate all generated assets against a standardized checklist verifying logo usage, color palette, typography, Arabic text legibility, image rights, and CTA clarity.

Key Rule: Human-in-the-loop control — AI outputs are treated as drafts; human sign-off is strictly required prior to final publication.

Key Deliverable: Approved compact brand kit, finalized visual assets, and template suite.

Phase 5: Automation Implementation & Demo Setup

Activities: Build or simulate a connected automation pipeline using n8n and Google Sheets to automate brief intake, AI prompt suggestions, approval status logging.

Key Deliverable: Fully demonstrable workflow integration/simulation for AI Prompt Library.
